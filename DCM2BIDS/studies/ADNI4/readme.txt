# <Name>

BIDS dataset created by catDCM2BIDS (CAT), including the evaluation of the MRI protocols, anonymization, and quality control.

## Overview

The Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) is a longitudinal multisite study of clinical, imaging, genetic, and biochemical biomarkers of Alzheimer's disease in cognitively normal older adults and participants with mild cognitive impairment (MCI) and Alzheimer's disease (AD). ADNI4 continues the former phases (ADNI1, ADNI-GO, ADNI2, ADNI3) with a focus on participant diversity, digital and blood-based screening, and a community-engaged recruitment.

## MRI

3T MRI of GE, Philips, and Siemens scanners with the harmonized ADNI4 protocol: T1w MPRAGE/IR-FSPGR, 3D FLAIR, 3D T2w, multi-echo T2*-weighted GRE, diffusion MRI, resting-state fMRI, and arterial spin labeling (depending on the site and visit).

## Participants and phenotypic data

Sex, research group of the enrollment (CN, SMC, EMCI, MCI, LMCI, AD), ADNI phase, and visit of the IDA search table of the images.

## Usage

The data are subject to the ADNI Data Use Agreement, i.e. they must not be redistributed. Please use the ADNI acknowledgement text and funding statement in publications (see https://adni.loni.usc.edu).
