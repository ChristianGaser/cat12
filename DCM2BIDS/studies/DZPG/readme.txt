# <Name>

BIDS dataset created by catDCM2BIDS (CAT), including the evaluation of the MRI protocols, anonymization, and quality control.

## Overview

The German Center for Mental Health (Deutsches Zentrum fuer Psychische Gesundheit, DZPG) is a research network of several sites in Germany for the research of mental health and mental disorders (https://www.dzpg.org).

## MRI

3T Siemens MRI of the DZPG imaging sites with the harmonized DZPG protocols: T1w MPRAGE (0.8 mm), T2w SPACE, diffusion MRI (standard and advanced protocol, 1.5 mm), resting-state fMRI (2 mm, AP and PA), and field maps.

## Participants and phenotypic data

Organization, substudy (e.g. JE1), analysis group, sex, and handedness of the phenotype table (see phenotype.json).

## Usage

Please check the data use rules of the DZPG and of the substudy before sharing the data.
