## Overview

The Nathan Kline Institute - Rockland Sample (NKI-RS) is a community-ascertained, lifespan sample (4-85 years) of Rockland County, New York, with deep phenotyping and multimodal MRI, to accelerate the discovery science in psychiatry. The initial release (NKI-RS1, 207 participants in weekly releases NKI.001-039) is shared by the International Neuroimaging Data-sharing Initiative (INDI) of the 1000 Functional Connectomes Project.

## MRI

One session per participant on a 3T Siemens TrioTim: T1w MPRAGE, resting-state fMRI, diffusion MRI (64 directions and 6 directions), field maps, and T2*-weighted images (see the NKI protocol documents of INDI).

## Participants and phenotypic data

Sex, age, handedness, BMI, race, ethnicity, and a broad battery of psychiatric, cognitive, and physical assessments (see NKI_Measures_Table_20101001.pdf of INDI).
