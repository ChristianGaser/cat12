# <Name>

BIDS dataset created by catDCM2BIDS (CAT), including the evaluation of the MRI protocols, anonymization, and quality control.

## Overview

The NKI-RS multiband test-retest pilot (NKI-TRT) of the Nathan Kline Institute - Rockland Sample (NKI-RS) was acquired to evaluate the reliability of multiband resting-state fMRI and diffusion MRI before the start of the Enhanced NKI-RS. The participants were primarily recruited from the initial NKI-RS and were not excluded for a history of psychiatric illness. The data are shared by the International Neuroimaging Data-sharing Initiative (INDI) of the 1000 Functional Connectomes Project.

## MRI

Two sessions per participant on a 3T Siemens TrioTim (32-channel head coil) with multiband sequences of the CMRR (University of Minnesota):

- T1w MPRAGE
- resting-state fMRI with TR = 645 ms (3 mm, 10 min), TR = 1400 ms (2 mm, 10 min), and a standard EPI with TR = 2500 ms (3 mm, 5 min)
- diffusion MRI with 137 directions (2 mm)
- task fMRI: visual checkerboard, eye movement calibration, and breath holding

## Participants and phenotypic data

Sex, age, handedness, and current/lifetime psychiatric diagnoses (DSM-IV) of the INDI phenotypic table.
