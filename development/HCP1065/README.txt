HCP-1065 population-probability tract atlas (Yeh, 2022)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source of the CAT atlas hcp1065 (see Atlas_HCP1065toCAT.m and 
templates_MNI152NLin2009cAsym/hcp1065.txt): the probability maps of the 64 
tracts (prob/*.nii.gz, 1 mm, ICBM 2009a nonlinear asymmetric space) of

  https://github.com/data-others/atlas/releases/download/hcp1065/hcp1065_prob_coverage_nifti.zip

(release hcp1065 of https://brain.labsolver.org/hcp_trk_atlas.html, 16.5 MB).
The maps are not part of CAT; Atlas_HCP1065toCAT downloads them if this 
directory has no prob subdirectory.
