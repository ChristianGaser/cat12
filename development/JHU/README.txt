JHU ICBM-DTI-81 white-matter labels (as distributed by FSL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source files of the CAT atlas jhu81 (see Atlas_JHU2CAT12.m and 
templates_MNI152NLin2009cAsym/jhu81.txt):

  JHU-ICBM-labels-1mm.nii.gz  labels in the MNI152 space of FSL (1 mm)
  JHU-labels.xml              names of the labels

from data/atlases of FSL (conda package fsl-data_atlases 2103.0). The 
conversion also needs the MNI152_T1_1mm.nii.gz of FSL (data/standard, 
conda package fsl-data_standard), which is not part of CAT.

The atlas was created by the Laboratory of Brain Anatomical MRI (S. Mori), 
Johns Hopkins University, from the DTI of 81 subjects of the ICBM DTI 
workgroup. It is released with FSL under the terms of the FSL license, i.e.
not for commercial use (contact the atlas coordinators for commercial use).

  Mori S, Wakana S, Nagae-Poetscher LM, van Zijl PCM (2005) MRI Atlas of 
  Human White Matter. Elsevier, Amsterdam.
  Wakana S, Caprihan A, Panzenboeck MM, et al. (2007) Reproducibility of 
  quantitative tractography methods applied to cerebral white matter. 
  NeuroImage 36(3):630-644.
  Hua K, Zhang J, Wakana S, et al. (2008) Tract probability maps in 
  stereotaxic spaces: analyses of white matter anatomy and tract-specific 
  quantification. NeuroImage 39(1):336-347.
