FreeSurfer parcellations of the MNI152NLin2009cAsym template (Lotter, 2025)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source files of the CAT atlases lotter2025_aparc, lotter2025_DKT, and 
lotter2025_a2009s (see Atlas_Lotter2025toCAT.m and the atlas text files in 
templates_MNI152NLin2009cAsym):

  aparc+aseg.nii.gz           Desikan-Killiany cortex and FreeSurfer aseg
  aparc.DKTatlas+aseg.nii.gz  Desikan-Killiany-Tourville cortex and aseg
  aparc.a2009s+aseg.nii.gz    Destrieux cortex and aseg

FreeSurfer recon-all (version 8.0.0) of the MNI152NLin2009cAsym T1w template, 
converted with mri_convert (FreeSurfer labels in the conformed 256^3 grid with 
MNI coordinates), from parcellations/MNI152NLin2009cAsym of

  Lotter LD (2025) FreeSurfer parcellations in MNI space. G-Node. 
  https://doi.org/10.12751/g-node.2mnxpm
  https://gin.g-node.org/doi/mni_freesurfer
