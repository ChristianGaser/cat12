HCP-1065 population-probability tract atlas:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This atlas is based on the population-probability tract atlas of Yeh (2022, 
HCP-1065, A): the probability maps of 64 white matter tracts (association, 
commissural, and projection tracts of the cerebrum, with subcomponents of the
cingulum, SLF, corticopontine, corticostriatal, and thalamic tracts) of the 
automatic tractography of the diffusion MRI of 1065 young adults of the Human 
Connectome Project (HCP) in ICBM 2009a nonlinear asymmetric space. 

For CAT, the probability maps were sampled in MNI152NLin2009cAsym space (the 
same coordinate system), and each voxel was labeled by the tract with the 
highest probability (winner takes all, at least 1%). As CAT extracts the 
regional WM values by the individual WM segmentation, all voxels with a WM 
probability of the CAT template above 1% but CSF/background below 50% were 
labeled to keep highly individual WM (e.g. of the gyri), i.e. 89% of the WM 
of the template (97% above z = -20 mm; the cerebellum and most of the 
brainstem are not labeled). Hence, the labels also cover the cortex and deep
GM and are only meant for WM measures (or WM-masked maps). Small tracts 
that run within larger tracts get only few voxels (e.g. the corticobulbar 
tract within the corticospinal tract), and the corpus callosum body also 
covers the lateral parts of its fibers in the centrum semiovale (see 
development/Atlas_HCP1065toCAT.m). The abbreviations are those of DSI Studio 
with a leading l/r/b for the hemisphere.
