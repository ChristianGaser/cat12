JHU ICBM-DTI-81 white-matter labels:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This atlas is based on the "JHU ICBM-DTI-81 White-Matter Labels" (A) of the 
Laboratory of Brain Anatomical MRI, Johns Hopkins University, as distributed 
with FSL (B): 48 white matter tract labels (50 with the tapetum) by hand 
segmentation of the average of the diffusion tensor maps of 81 subjects of the
ICBM DTI workgroup (mean age 39 years, range 18-59, 42 males and 39 females).
It labels the deep white matter (core of the tracts), i.e. not the superficial
white matter.

CAT12 was used to segment the MNI152 T1 template of FSL (MNI152_T1_1mm, the 
space of the atlas) and to estimate the normalization parameters to transform 
the label map to MNI152NLin2009cAsym space (nearest neighbor, see 
development/Atlas_JHU2CAT12.m). The label IDs and names are those of FSL, the 
colors were added for CAT (one per tract in both hemispheres).

License: The atlas is released with FSL under the terms of the FSL license, 
i.e. it should not be used for commercial purposes (please contact the atlas 
coordinators for commercial use).
