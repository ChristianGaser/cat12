FreeSurfer parcellation of the MNI152NLin2009cAsym template (Lotter, 2025):
Desikan-Killiany-Tourville (aparc.DKTatlas, 62 cortical regions; Klein and Tourville, 2012)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FreeSurfer recon-all (version 8.0.0) was run on the MNI152NLin2009cAsym T1w
template by Lotter (2025). This atlas combines the cortical parcellation with
the FreeSurfer segmentation (aseg: subcortical structures, cerebellum, 
brainstem, ventral diencephalon, ventricles, cerebral white matter, corpus 
callosum, and CSF) with the FreeSurfer label IDs and the names and colors of 
the FreeSurferColorLUT, e.g. for comparisons with FreeSurfer or FastSurfer.

CAT version (see development/Atlas_Lotter2025toCAT.m): The FreeSurfer labels
were sampled (nearest neighbor) in the grid of the CAT atlases (1 mm). The 
FreeSurfer cortex of the template is too tight for the ROI estimation of CAT 
(only 66% of the GM of the CAT shooting template in GM labels). Therefore, 
voxels of the template GM (GM >= WM and GM > 0.1) up to 4 mm from the 
FreeSurfer cortex that were labeled as background, CSF, or cerebral white 
matter got the label of the closest cortex voxel, i.e. 87% of the template GM 
are in GM labels (as for the neuromorphometrics atlas). The subcortical 
labels were not changed. Lotter (2025) provides a smoothed version of the 
cortical labels in a liberal cortical mask (Schaefer 2018), whereas this 
version keeps the borders of the FreeSurfer labels and also the non-cortical
aseg labels (cerebellum, brainstem, white matter, ventricles, and CSF).
Please note that the Destrieux parcellation separates gyri and sulci, which 
fits less to the averaged MNI template.
